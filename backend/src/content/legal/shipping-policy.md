## 1. Where we deliver
We deliver to addresses **anywhere in India**. We don't ship outside India at the moment. Please enter a complete address with a correct 6-digit pincode and a 10-digit mobile number, so the courier can reach you.

## 2. Delivery time
- Orders are usually delivered **within 4–7 business days** after confirmation. A Cash on Delivery order is confirmed when you place it; an online order is confirmed when your payment succeeds.
- This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Delays can happen, for example during festivals, bad weather, or courier and network disruptions. We will keep you updated.

## 3. Delivery and Cash on Delivery charges
- **Free delivery** when the items in your order total **₹500 or more**.
- Below ₹500, a delivery charge of **₹50** applies.
- **Cash on Delivery** has an extra charge of **₹50** and is available for orders up to **₹5,000**. Larger orders can be paid online.
- All charges are shown on the checkout page **before** you pay or place the order. There are no hidden charges.

## 4. Shipping and tracking
- We pack your order and hand it to a trusted courier partner.
- When it ships, we send you the **courier name and tracking number** by email and WhatsApp. You can also see them, with a **Track on courier website** link, in **My Account → My Orders**, or on the [Track Order](/track-order) page using your order number and mobile number or email.
- Every step (confirmed, packed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered) is shown with its date and time.

## 5. Receiving your order
- Please check the parcel when it arrives. If it is **damaged, defective or the wrong item**, tell us **within 7 days of delivery** with clear photos, from **My Orders → Return / Exchange**. We will replace it, or refund the item price **plus the delivery and Cash on Delivery charges**.
- For **Cash on Delivery**, please keep the exact order amount ready to pay the courier.

## 6. If delivery is unsuccessful
If the courier can't deliver your order (for example the address is incomplete or nobody is available), the parcel comes back to us and **we will contact you**.
- **Paid online:** you get a **full refund** after we contact you.
- **Cash on Delivery:** nothing is charged.

## 7. Changing or cancelling an order
- You can **cancel** any time **before the order is shipped**, from **My Orders**. There is no cancellation charge.
- If you need to correct the delivery address, please contact us as soon as possible. We can help only **before the order is shipped**.

## 8. Returns and exchanges
Returns and exchanges are possible **within 7 days of delivery** for products marked returnable. **Return pickup is free**: once we approve your request, we arrange for the courier to collect the item from your address. See our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/policies/refunds) for the full rules.
