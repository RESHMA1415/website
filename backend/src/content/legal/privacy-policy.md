Some of our service providers may store or process data on servers outside India. The DPDP Act permits this, except to countries the Government of India restricts. We use only reputable providers that protect data with appropriate safeguards.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- **Account and profile data:** while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we erase this data unless the law requires us to keep it. We may close and erase accounts that have been inactive for 3 years, after telling you at least 48 hours in advance.
- **Orders, invoices and payment records:** for as long as tax, accounting and consumer laws require (generally up to 8 years), even if your account is deleted.
- **Security logs:** at least 1 year, as the DPDP Rules require, and then deleted.
- **Reviews:** while they are published. You can ask us to remove your review at any time.
- **Cash on Delivery refund details (UPI/bank):** only until the refund is completed and recorded.

## 7. How we protect your data

We take reasonable security safeguards as required by the DPDP Act and the IT Act. These include:
- passwords stored only in encrypted (hashed) form;
- secure (HTTPS) connections;
- limited access to staff who need the data;
- login protection (an account locks for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords);
- monitoring and security logs.

Payments are processed by Razorpay, which follows the PCI-DSS card security standard. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform you and the **Data Protection Board of India** as the DPDP Act requires.

## 8. Children

The Platform is for people aged **18 years or older**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete the data.

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

1. **Access:** ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it, and of the processors we have shared it with.
2. **Correction and updating:** correct, complete or update your data. Most details can be changed yourself in **My Account**.
3. **Erasure:** ask us to delete your personal data, except data the law requires us to keep.
4. **Withdraw consent:** at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before. We may then be unable to provide some services, such as order updates.
5. **Grievance redressal:** complain to our Grievance Officer (Section 11) about how your data is handled.
6. **Nominate:** name another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email shopmesupport2@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 80891 11500 from your registered email or mobile number. We may need to verify your identity first. We will respond **within 30 days**.
