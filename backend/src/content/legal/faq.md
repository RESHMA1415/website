## Orders and payment

### How do I place an order?
Choose your size and colour, tap **Add to Cart** (or **Buy Now**), then go to **Checkout**. Choose or add your delivery address, pick how you'd like to pay, and place the order. You need a SHOPME account to check out, so we can keep you updated and you can track your order.

### Which payment methods do you accept?
- **Online:** UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others), debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets, through our secure payment partner **Razorpay**.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** pay the courier when your order arrives.

### Is paying online safe?
Yes. Payments are handled by **Razorpay**. Your card, UPI and bank details go directly to Razorpay; SHOPME never sees or stores them.

### Is Cash on Delivery available?
Yes, for orders up to **₹5,000**. COD has an extra charge of **₹50**, shown at checkout. Please keep the exact amount ready for the courier.

### My payment failed but money was deducted. What now?
Don't worry. If money was deducted for a payment that didn't go through, your bank or Razorpay returns it automatically, usually within **5–7 business days**. You can also try again with **Retry Payment** on the order.

### Do you have coupons or discount codes?
Not at the moment. The prices you see already include our discounts.

## Delivery

### Where do you deliver?
**Anywhere in India.** We don't ship outside India yet.

### How long does delivery take?
Usually **4–7 business days** after your order is confirmed. See our [Shipping Policy](/policies/shipping) for details.

### How much is delivery?
**Free** when your items total **₹500 or more**. Below ₹500, delivery costs **₹50**.

### How can I track my order?
When your order ships, we send you the courier name and tracking number by email and WhatsApp. You can also track it in **My Account → My Orders**, or on the [Track Order](/track-order) page with your order number and mobile number or email.

## Cancellations

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, any time **before it is shipped**, free of charge. Go to **My Orders**, open the order and choose **Cancel Order**.

### When will I get my money back after cancelling?
For online payments, we start a **full refund** straight away to your original payment method. It usually reaches you within **5–7 business days**. For Cash on Delivery orders, nothing was charged.

## Returns, exchanges and refunds

### What is your return policy?
You can return or exchange products marked returnable **within 7 days of delivery**. Items must be unused and unwashed, with their original tags and packaging. Full details are in our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/policies/refunds).

### How do I return or exchange an item?
Go to **My Orders**, open the delivered order and choose **Return / Exchange**. Pick the items, the reason, and Return or Exchange. We review requests within **2 business days**.

### Do I have to pay for the return pickup?
**No. Return pickup is free.** Once we approve your request, we arrange for the courier to collect the item from your address.

### Which items can't be returned?
**Beauty and personal-care products** (such as makeup and skincare) can't be returned for hygiene reasons, unless they arrive damaged, defective or wrong. The product page shows "Non-returnable" for these items.

### I received a damaged or wrong item. What should I do?
Request a return within **7 days of delivery** and add clear photos of the item and packaging. We will replace it, or refund the item price **plus the delivery and COD charges**.

### Can I exchange for a different size or colour?
Yes, for a different size or colour of the same product, if it's in stock. If the new one costs less, we refund the difference. If it costs more, please return the item and place a new order.

### How long do refunds take?
We start your refund within **2 business days** after the returned item passes its quality check. It then usually reaches you within **5–7 business days**.
- **Paid online:** refunded to your original payment method.
- **Cash on Delivery:** refunded to the UPI ID or bank account you give us in the return request.

## Sizes and products

### How do I find my size?
Open **Size Chart** on the product page. It shows the measurements for that product. If you're between sizes, choose the larger one.

### Are the colours in the photos accurate?
We photograph products as accurately as we can, but colours can look slightly different on different screens. If the colour you receive is clearly different from the picture, you can return it within 7 days.

## Your account

### I forgot my password. What do I do?
Choose **Forgot password?** on the login page and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new password. The link works for 30 minutes.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to **My Account → Profile → Your Data & Privacy** and choose **Delete my account**. Orders still on their way must be delivered or closed first. Read more in our [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy).

## Reviews

### Who can write a review?
Only customers who bought the product and received it. Open the delivered order in **My Orders** and choose **Write a Review**. You can add up to 5 photos and a short video.

### Why isn't my review showing yet?
Every review is checked before it appears, usually within a few days. You can see its status in **My Account → My Reviews**.
