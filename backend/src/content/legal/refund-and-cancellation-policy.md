## 1. Cancelling an order

### 1.1 Cancellation by you
- You can cancel an order **at any time before it is shipped**. Go to **My Account → My Orders**, open the order, choose **Cancel Order** and a reason. You can also contact us on WhatsApp or by email with your order number.
- **There is no cancellation charge.**
- Once an order has been **shipped**, it can't be cancelled. You can return eligible products after delivery (Section 2).

### 1.2 Cancellation by SHOPME
We may cancel an order in these cases:
- the product is out of stock;
- the product was listed at a wrong price;
- we can't deliver to the address;
- the order appears fraudulent or breaks our Terms & Conditions.

We will tell you the reason by email or WhatsApp, and refund in full any amount you paid.

### 1.3 Refund on cancellation
| How you paid | Refund |
|---|---|
| Online (UPI, card, net banking, wallet) | **Full amount paid**, including delivery charges, to the original payment method |
| Cash on Delivery | Nothing to refund, because no payment has been taken |

## 2. Returns and exchanges

### 2.1 Return window
You can request a return or exchange **within 7 days of the delivery date** from **My Account → My Orders → Return / Exchange**. You can return some or all of the items in an order.

### 2.2 Eligible products
A product can be returned if:
- it is marked **returnable** on its product page;
- it is unused, unwashed and undamaged, with all original tags, labels and packaging;
- it is returned with any free items that came with it.

### 2.3 Non-returnable products
These can't be returned or exchanged unless they arrive **damaged, defective or wrong**:
- **beauty and personal-care products** (for example makeup, skincare and fragrance), for hygiene reasons;
- any other product marked **"Non-returnable"** on its product page.

### 2.4 Damaged, defective or wrong items
If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong item, please report it **within 7 days of delivery**. Include **clear photos** of the item and the packaging; photos are required for these requests. We will replace the item, or refund the full item price **plus the delivery and Cash on Delivery charges**.

### 2.5 How a return works
1. **Request:** choose the items and quantity, Return or Exchange, and a reason. For Cash on Delivery orders, give the UPI ID or bank details for your refund.
2. **Review:** we approve or reject the request, usually **within 2 business days**. If we reject it, we tell you why.
3. **Pickup:** for approved requests, we arrange for the courier to collect the item from your address. Please keep it packed and ready.
4. **Quality check:** when the item reaches us, we check its condition. If it doesn't meet Section 2.2, we may decline the return and send the item back to you.
5. **Refund or replacement:** we process your refund (Section 3) or ship your exchange item.

You will get updates by email and WhatsApp at every step.

### 2.6 Exchanges
- You can exchange a product for **a different size or colour of the same product**, subject to stock.
- **Same price:** no extra cost.
- **Lower price:** we refund the difference.
- **Higher price:** an exchange isn't possible. Please return the item for a refund and place a new order.
- **Not in stock:** you can choose a refund instead.

## 3. Refunds

### 3.1 How much is refunded
| Situation | Refund amount |
|---|---|
| Order cancelled before shipping (paid online) | Full amount paid |
| Order cancelled by SHOPME | Full amount paid |
| Return of some or all items (normal return) | Selling price of the returned items. Delivery and Cash on Delivery charges are not refunded |
| Damaged, defective or wrong item | Full item price **plus** delivery and Cash on Delivery charges |
| Exchange for a lower-priced size/colour | The price difference |
| Payment taken but order not confirmed (for example the item sold out while you were paying, or a duplicate payment) | Full amount paid, **refunded automatically** |
| Prepaid order that could not be delivered and came back to us | Full amount paid, after we contact you |

### 3.2 How refunds are paid
- **Orders paid online:** refunded to your **original payment method** (the same UPI ID, card, bank account or wallet) through our payment partner, Razorpay. We can't refund to a different account.
- **Cash on Delivery orders:** refunded by bank transfer (IMPS/NEFT) or UPI to the **UPI ID or bank account** you give us (account holder name, account number and IFSC). Please check these details carefully. We are not responsible for delays caused by incorrect details.

### 3.3 Refund timelines
- We **start** the refund **within 2 business days** of the order being cancelled, or of the returned item passing its quality check.
- The money usually reaches your account **within 5–7 business days** after that, depending on your bank or payment provider.
- **Failed payments:** if money was deducted for a payment that failed, it is returned automatically by your bank or Razorpay, usually within 5–7 business days. No request is needed.
- We will send you the refund amount and reference number by email or WhatsApp.

If you haven't received your refund after these timelines, please contact us with your order number and we will help you trace it.

## 4. Shipping charges at a glance

- Cash on Delivery has an extra **₹50** charge and is available on orders up to **₹5,000**.
- These charges are shown before you pay. They are refunded only as described in Section 3.1.
